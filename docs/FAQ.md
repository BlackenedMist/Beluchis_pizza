# Beluchi's Pizzas — Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering

**How do I order?**
Go to the website, tap **ORDER NOW!** or **MENU**, choose your dishes and sizes, then tap **Checkout**. Follow the three steps (details → delivery/collection → payment).

**Can I order when you are closed?**
You can browse the menu and build your cart any time, but checkout only works during opening hours (10:00 – 21:00). The cart is saved on your device.

**Do you deliver?**
Yes, within roughly 10 km of Worcester. Free delivery within 3 km on food orders from R200; R85 up to 10 km; R95 beyond 10 km. Drop a pin on the map at checkout for an accurate fee.

**What if I don't enter an address?**
Your order is treated as **collection**.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Cash on delivery, card on delivery, pay at counter, and (when enabled) paid online via PayGate.

**Can I use a coupon?**
Yes. Enter the code in the coupon field at checkout (Step 3). Personalised offers also appear in your portal.

**How do I reorder a previous order?**
Log in to **My Orders**, find the order, and tap **Reorder**. The same items are added to your cart.

## Account

**Do I need an account to order?**
No. You can order as a guest using your cellphone number. To track orders and save favourites, create an account afterwards via "Claim guest account".

**I ordered as a guest — how do I see my orders?**
Go to **My Orders**, tap "Claim guest account", enter the cellphone you ordered with, your first name, and choose a password. Your past orders will appear.

**I forgot my password.**
Use "Forgot password" on the portal — enter your cellphone and first name, then set a new password.

**How many delivery locations can I save?**
Up to 3. Each needs a name and a pin on the map.

**What are favourites?**
Tap the heart (♡) on any dish or deal to save it. Favourites are stored on your account and appear in the portal.

**What is the Napoletana base?**
A 48-hour fermented dough for a chewy, airy crust with puffy edges, available on medium pizzas at an extra R20. Made in batches, so it may run out for the day.
